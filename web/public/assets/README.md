# Renovo Painting Solutions - Asset Directory Structure

Drop your real company assets into the corresponding subfolders below:

- `brand/` -> Official logos (`.svg`, `.png`), favicons, brand manual, and color palette references.
- `kitchens/` -> Photos of custom kitchen manufacturing, cabinet installations, islands, and shop/CNC photos.
- `painting-interior/` -> Photos of interior residential painting, trim, walls, ceilings, and cabinet refinishing.
- `painting-exterior/` -> Photos of exterior house painting, siding, brick/stucco coatings, and entry doors.
- `before-after/` -> Paired Before & After transformation photos.
- `profiles-and-samples/` -> Close-ups of door styles (Shaker, Slab, Fluted), wood grains, hardware, and paint swatches.
- `blueprints-and-docs/` -> CAD sketches, PDF catalogs, spec sheets, or videos.
